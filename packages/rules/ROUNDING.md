# Rounding

Every figure the engine produces follows one of these rules. The site never rounds a figure itself; it formats what the engine returns.

| Figure | Rule | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Taxable income | Gross pay less the allowance (plus any K code addition), rounded down to a whole pound | HMRC rounds taxable income down to the pound in Self Assessment computations |
| Income tax in each band | The slice times the rate, to the penny, half up | The annual figure HMRC's own estimate gives; a monthly payslip may differ by pence because PAYE works period by period |
| Allowance taper | £1 lost for every £2 above the start, the reduction rounded down to a pound, never below zero | gov.uk: income over £100,000 |
| Tax code allowance | The code number times 10 (1257L gives £12,570) | HMRC's PAYE tables treat 1257 as £12,579 for monthly pay; the annual difference is under £2 of tax and the gov.uk figure is used |
| K code cap | Tax taken cannot exceed half of pay | gov.uk: K codes |
| National Insurance | The slice between the annual thresholds times the rate, to the penny | Annual thresholds from the employer rates page; directors use this annual method, employees are assessed per pay period and may differ by pence |
| Student loan | The rate times pay above the threshold, rounded down to a whole pound | Student loan deductions are rounded down to the pound in each pay period |
| Pension Tax Allowance | 20% of the gap between the full new State Pension and the personal allowance, rounded up to a pound; withdrawn at £1 for every £3 above the full new State Pension | HMRC's explainer gives "up to £94" for a gap of £466.40 |
| Monthly, weekly and daily | The annual figure divided by 12, 52 and 260, to the penny | Convention; a daily figure assumes a five-day week |
| Marginal rate | One minus the change in take-home over the next £1,000, to two decimal places | Convention |

Figures are shown with two decimals in the ledger and none in headlines. Thousands always carry a separator.
