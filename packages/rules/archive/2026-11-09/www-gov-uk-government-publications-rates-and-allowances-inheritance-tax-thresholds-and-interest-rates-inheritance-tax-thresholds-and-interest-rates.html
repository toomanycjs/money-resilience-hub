Inheritance Tax interest rates from October 1988

Interest rates change from time to time along with market rates. This section shows interest periods and rates since October 1988.

You can use this information to calculate how much interest is due on an Inheritance Tax payment by using the Inheritance Tax interest calculator.

You can pay your Inheritance Tax in yearly instalments.

Find out more information on how Inheritance Tax works.

If you pay too much Inheritance Tax in advance, HMRC will refund any excess money to you, plus interest on the amount you’ve overpaid. This rate of interest is displayed in the ‘Interest on repayments’ column in the following table.
